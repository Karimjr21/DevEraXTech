# Brag Plan: DevEraXTech (TikTok brand-awareness cut)

Run: full /brag (user asked for "full brag"), vertical, no voiceover.
User direction: realistic, professional, luxurious, premium TikTok for brand awareness; 3 trending tech
questions; 3 elite hooks; question 1 is answered only at the very end, with the whole video making the
viewer feel the answer is seconds away; on-screen and caption keywords that help TikTok search/For You.

## What is this app?
DevEraXTech is a Cairo web and app development studio ("We build premium web & app experiences") that
builds websites, online stores, mobile apps, Windows software and AI integrations, with care plans.

## The angle
A luxury "question chamber". Three questions the tech world keeps typing into TikTok search, asked in
gold Playfair on near-black. A thin gold **answer meter** sits under every question and keeps filling
(18% → 41% → 67% → 94% → 99%) but never completes, so the viewer is always one beat away from the
answer to Question 1. Q2 and Q3 get fast, satisfying answers (proof the video pays off), which makes
the unpaid Q1 itch harder. At 100% the meter flashes and Q1 is answered, then DevEraXTech's real work
(Nilora Estates) and logo close it.

## Three hooks (elite, each a pattern interrupt)
- **Hook 1 (0.0s, opening):** "This question is splitting the tech world in two." (per-word, gold on
  "two"). A curiosity gap plus conflict, lands before the first beat. It sets up Q1.
- **Hook 2 (9.3s, re-hook before Q2):** "Most agencies will never tell you this." Insider-secret framing
  resets attention at the 9s drop-off point.
- **Hook 3 (17.5s, re-hook before Q3):** "Your competitors already know this one." FOMO/loss framing at
  the second drop-off point.
- **Tension beat (26.2s):** "Don't scroll. It's loading." The meter hits 99% and stalls. This holds the
  viewer for the payoff (not a fourth hook, a retention lock).

## The three trending questions
1. **"Will AI replace web developers?"** Asked at 4.4s and answered only at 28.4s. Every scene keeps a small
   "Q1 · ANSWER LOADING" meter on screen, so it always feels next.
2. **"Does your business need an AI assistant in 2026?"** Answered on the spot with DevEraXTech's own
   service lines: Website chatbots · WhatsApp assistants · Automated lead capture.
3. **"Why is Google ignoring your website?"** Answered on the spot: Core Web Vitals · Page speed ·
   Schema markup (from the SEO & Performance service).

## Payoff (Q1 answer)
"Will AI replace web developers?" → **"No."** (slam on the beat) → "AI writes code. It can't design
what your brand stands for." → verbatim site line: **"Premium design, not templates."** → real proof:
the Nilora Estates site DevEraXTech built ("Where the river meets the light.").

## Outro
Gold logo, "DevEraXTech", verbatim "We build premium web & app experiences", "Secure · Scalable ·
Pixel-perfect", CTA "Follow for Part 2 →" (a series hook so the account gets the follow).

## User flow worth showing
None, because the subject is a studio site. Real material instead: the gold logo, the site's hero copy and
"Why DevEraXTech" line, the services data (AI integrations, SEO), and the Nilora Estates portfolio shot.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic, with polished restraint
- Creative direction: luxury watch-commercial meets tech explainer; black, gold, slow confident type
- Interpretation: big serif type, generous holds, soft blur-ins, no bounce, few but heavy SFX; pace
  comes from scene changes every 4–8s and the meter, never from flashing text.

## Format: vertical, 1080x1920 at 30fps
## Duration: 40s
The brag law says 15–25s. The user's brief (3 hooks + 3 questions + delayed answer) can't land in 25s
without breaking reading time. 40s is inside TikTok's best completion band for educational content.

## Safe zones (TikTok UI)
Top 150px (tabs), bottom 480px (caption/sound bar), right 140px (action rail). Key text lives in
x 90–940, y 260–1400.

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0a0a0a
- Accent: #d4af37, gradient #d4af37 → #f5d573 → #d4af37 (`gold-gradient`)
- Text: #ffffff / gray-300 (#d1d5db)
- Display font: Playfair Display; Body: Inter
- Strongest visual: the 3D gold logo, gold-gradient headline text, the gold circuit motif

## SEO / algorithm keywords (on screen and in caption)
On-screen (TikTok OCRs text): "AI", "web developers", "AI assistant", "chatbots", "WhatsApp",
"Google", "SEO", "Core Web Vitals", "website". Caption + hashtags are in share-copy.txt.

## Share copy (draft)
Will AI replace web developers? We answered it, but only at the end. 🖤✨

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady and clean, 110 BPM), volume 0.32, fade out under
  the logo
- Music cue guidance: preset cues at 8.74, 17.47, 22.93 (strong). Scene seams snapped to beats:
  4.39, 9.29, 17.47, 26.2, 28.38. Beyond 25s, use the 0.545s grid.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass energy breathes the gold ambient glow and the meter's sheen.
  No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, heavy, luxurious. A deep bell on Hook 1's key word, soft impacts on question
  arrivals, light glass/plate ticks on answer chips, a glitch tick when the meter stalls, and a heavy
  bell on "No.".
- Restraint rule: never more than one SFX per 0.5s; no comedic sounds.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1: Hook 1, 0.0–4.39s
Black, a soft gold glow, gold dust. "This question is splitting" / "the tech world in two." per-word
blur-in, "two" in gold gradient; a gold hairline splits the frame horizontally under it.
Audio: deep bell under "two". Transition: the split line widens into Q1 (object becomes world).

### Scene 2: Question 1, 4.39–9.29s
Small label "QUESTION 01". Big serif "Will AI replace / web developers?" with "AI" in gold. Below it the
answer meter appears: "ANSWER LOADING" 0 → 18% and stalls with a flicker.
Sequential: the label, then the title, then the meter. Audio: soft impact on the title, then a glitch tick on the stall.
Transition: whip up.

### Scene 3: Hook 2 + Question 2, 9.29–17.47s
"Most agencies will never tell you this." (hold 1.9s) → "QUESTION 02 / Does your business need an AI
assistant in 2026?" → 3 answer chips arrive one by one (every other beat): Website chatbots · WhatsApp
assistants · Automated lead capture. The mini Q1 meter at the top keeps climbing to 41%.
Audio: glass ticks on the chips.

### Scene 4: Hook 3 + Question 3, 17.47–26.2s
"Your competitors already know this one." → "QUESTION 03 / Why is Google ignoring your website?" →
a Core Web Vitals panel: three rows tick to "PASS" (Core Web Vitals · Page speed · Schema markup).
The mini meter climbs to 67%, then 94%.

### Scene 5: Tension, 26.2–28.38s
Everything clears. The meter grows big and centered: "Q1 · Will AI replace web developers?" 94 → 99%,
stalls, and a glitch flicker plays. Then "Don't scroll." Audio: the music thins (duck) and a riser-like dice shake.

### Scene 6: The answer, 28.38–35.0s
The meter hits 100% and flashes to gold. "No." slams huge (bell). "AI writes code." / "It can't design
what your brand stands for." → "Premium design, not templates." over the Nilora Estates site card
sliding up ("Nilora Estates · built by DevEraXTech").

### Scene 7: Outro, 35.0–40.0s
Gold logo resolves from blur, "DevEraXTech", "We build premium web & app experiences", "Secure ·
Scalable · Pixel-perfect", "Follow for Part 2 →". Music fades out. Last line holds 2s+.

**Music mood:** cinematic-steady
**Audio summary:** a steady bed under weighted hits that tightens into a near-silent stall, then a bell on "No." and a clean fade under the logo.
