## Hooks
1. 0.0s, reverse psychology: "If you already know the answer, delete this video." (highlighter sweep)
2. 9.5s, save-driver: "Screenshot this one." (crop marks + shutter flash). Saves are a strong ranking signal.
3. 17.5s, loss aversion: "Get this wrong, lose the customer." (stamped on the strong cue)

## Questions
1. "Is ChatGPT killing Google search?" Answered only at 28s. The answer is redacted and stamped
   CLASSIFIED; then a 6-tile word decodes one letter per file (S → C → H → E). At 26s the music cuts out
   on "4/6 DECODED · Comment your guess." (comment driver), then the drop returns as M and A flip:
   SCHEMA. "No. But it's changing who gets found. Schema tells Google and AI who you are."
2. "Can AI build your business website in a weekend?" A comparison table: Looks finished ✓✓ ·
   Security from day one ✗✓ · Scales as you grow ✗✓ (from "Security built in from day one" and
   "a structure that can grow").
3. "App or WhatsApp? What does your business need?" A chat-assistant phone demo, then "Both. iOS +
   Android from one codebase + WhatsApp assistants" (services.json).

## Outro

"Follow for File 04 →", "Save this dossier."
