# DevEraXTech — "The Atelier" (luxury brand-awareness film)

**Status: provisional draft. The brand owner must approve it before publishing.**

- Format: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 32.0 s.
- Audio: -15 LUFS, -4.1 dBFS peak. Piano is mastered a little softer than the TikTok cuts.
- Files:
  - `atelier.mp4`: master.
  - `atelier-share.mp4`: 44.1 kHz copy for social.
  - `qa-sheet.jpg`: frame grid for review.

## Why this idea is new
Earlier films covered:
- paper napkin
- question cards
- yellow Duo
- night-sky POV
- photo table
- news hinge story
- Black Friday countdown
- the earlier brand films

This film is the first:
- with **no hook gimmicks, no questions and no product demo**;
- to borrow the language of **luxury maison advertising**: chapter titles, a slow piano score, cinema bars, dissolves and a maker's mark;
- to sell the *brand idea* (craftsmanship) instead of a problem.

## Craft details
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond italic and Cinzel, both SIL Open Font License, bundled locally.
- **Colour:** gold #d9b968 and ivory #f7f2e8.
- **Picture:** cinema bars with "DEVERAXTECH" in the top bar the whole time, which builds brand recall. Graded footage with fine film grain, a lens vignette and slow push-ins.
- **Edit:** every shot dissolves into the next over 0.6 s, with cuts on the piano's bar lines.
- **Brands:** watch footage was framed to keep third-party engravings (watch-brand names) out of shot.

## Fact check
- The lines are metaphors about precision. They make no claims about clients, awards or numbers.
- "Milliseconds" refers to web performance, which really is measured in milliseconds.
