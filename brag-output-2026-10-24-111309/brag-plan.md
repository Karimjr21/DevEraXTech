# Brag Plan: DevEraXTech — "One Line." (film 2)

/brag full workflow + Hyperframes 0.8.190. Provisional; not approved for publication.

## What is this?
A DevEraXTech-only brand film. No client project, photos or UI recreations; it uses only the logo's own geometry.

## The angle
The DevEraXTech logo ends in circuit traces with ring terminals. One such trace becomes the whole film. In a single unbroken take it draws everything the studio builds, then lands back on one of the logo's own ring terminals. One line, everything we build, planned, designed and built end to end.

## How it differs from film 1 ("Engineered Excellence")
| | Film 1 | Film 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | A client build (Nilora Estates) | DevEraXTech's own service range |
| Look | Photo-real UI, 3D exploded layers | Pure gold line-art on black, one continuous stroke |
| Rhythm | Cinematic, ~110 BPM, long holds | Kinetic oner, 120 BPM, one section per bar (2 s) |
| Payoff | Three screens align on a seam | A rewind up the whole line, then the ring terminal flies into the logo |
Shared campaign system: brand row, Inter 800 titles, gold seam/ring terminal, the same three-note sonic signature on the logo.
