## The idea
DevEraXTech's real process, told on one sheet of paper. The site's own steps, from Home "How It Works: From first call to launch": **01 Discovery call. 02 Plan and design. 03 Build, secure and launch.** A client's scribble on a napkin becomes a wireframe, then a real app; it's built, sealed as secure, folded into a paper plane and launched. The logo is stamped on at the end.
